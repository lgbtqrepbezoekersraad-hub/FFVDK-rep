Who I Am & My Mandate

I serve as an independent LGBTQ+ Representative and Spokesperson acting for and on behalf of participation councils connected to De Regenboog Groep (DRG)—including the Locatieraad (LR), Bezoekersraad (BR), Deelnemersraad (DR), and Centrale Raad (CR).

Following alignment with Eric on role boundaries, my mandate centers on systemic advocacy, policy review, and community representation—explicitly separated from DRG management and incident logging. I also maintain GroovePark as a structurally distinct, independent community movement initiative while keeping clear boundaries across all responsibilities.

Core Responsibilities & Boundaries

Systemic Council Advocacy

Representing LGBTQ+ and council perspectives across LR, BR, DR, and CR meetings with a focus on structural and policy-level improvements rather than management operations.

Anonymized Signals & Research

Gathering community feedback through informal research and listening while strictly keeping council notes anonymized.

Warm Referrals & Clear Boundaries

Separating council representation from incident logging and casework, connecting individuals through warm referrals to professional channels such as the CVP.

Clear Documentation & Networks

Building external community partnerships and maintaining clear role documentation and coordinated communication across initiatives.

Initiatives & Community Work

  • GroovePark (Founder): An independent community movement initiative kept structurally distinct from council mandates while connecting with community wellness. Currently on a year-end break and relaunching in January 2027 on Sundays (18:00–21:30) at Buurtkamer Kostverloren. (Visit GroovePark)
  • Weekly Amsterdam Community Calendar: Curating and sharing a weekly overview of free and low-cost safe spaces, social gatherings, and local resources.
