Who I Am & My Mandate

I serve as a Representative and Spokesperson acting for and on behalf of the participation councils within De Regenboog Groep—including the Bezoekersraad (BR), Deelnemersraad (DR), Centrale Raad (CR), and Locatieraad (LR)—with a focus on LGBTQ+ inclusion, systemic improvements, and lived-experience advocacy.

Rather than operating as an independent intermediary with management, my role is grounded in formal council mandates: gathering community signals, translating on-the-ground feedback into structured policy input, and ensuring marginalized voices are heard accurately at the decision-making table.

Core Responsibilities

Council Representation

Synthesizing systemic talking points, reviewing policies, and representing visitor and participant perspectives across BR, DR, and CR meetings.

Informal Research & Signals

Listening to community experiences across Amsterdam, conducting informal research, and maintaining strict anonymization of all council notes.

Boundary-Aware Referrals

Navigating clear role boundaries—avoiding uncompensated casework while providing warm referrals to professional support such as the CVP.

Community Bridge-Building

Connecting local venues, grassroots initiatives, and outreach partners to expand safe, accessible spaces and low-threshold programming.

Community Initiatives

  • GroovePark (Founder): A community movement and wellness initiative under De Regenboog Groep. Currently on a year-end break and relaunching in January 2027 on Sundays (18:00–21:30) at Buurtkamer Kostverloren. (Visit GroovePark)
  • Amsterdam Community Calendar: Curating and sharing a weekly overview of free and low-cost safe spaces, social gatherings, and support resources across the city.
